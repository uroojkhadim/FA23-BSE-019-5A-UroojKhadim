# App Icon Creation Instructions

To create a custom app icon for your SMART POS application:

## Option 1: AI-Generated Icon Services
1. Use an AI image generator like:
   - Canva
   - Figma
   - Midjourney
   - DALL-E
   - Stable Diffusion

2. Describe your icon as:
   - "Professional point of sale app icon with shopping cart, barcode scanner, or cash register elements"
   - "Modern business POS system icon with clean, minimalist design"
   - "E-commerce POS app icon with blue/purple color scheme matching your app theme"

## Option 2: Design Specifications
Create an icon with these specifications:
- Size: 1024x1024 pixels (minimum)
- Format: PNG or JPEG
- Background: Preferably transparent or solid color
- Style: Material Design guidelines compliant
- Content: Include POS-related elements like shopping cart, barcode, cash register, or business symbols

## Recommended Design Elements:
- Shopping cart or basket
- Barcode scanner
- Cash register
- Money/financial symbols
- Business chart/graph
- Your brand colors (purple/blue theme from your app)

## After Creating Your Icon:
1. Save the icon as "app_icon.png" in this directory
2. Run the following command in your project root:
   flutter pub get
   flutter pub run flutter_launcher_icons:main
3. Your app icons will be generated automatically

## Color Suggestions:
- Primary: #673AB7 (Deep Purple)
- Secondary: #9C27B0 (Purple)
- Accent: #FF9800 (Orange) or #4CAF50 (Green)

This will replace the default Flutter launcher icon with your custom SMART POS icon.
